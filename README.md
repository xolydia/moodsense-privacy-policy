## MoodSense Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** April 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MoodSense collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the MoodSense mobile application.

## Who this applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of MoodSense. It covers information you provide directly, information generated through app usage, and information needed for app functionality and account access.

## Information collected

MoodSense may collect the following information:

- Account information such as your name, email address, and authentication-related information when you sign in.
- Self-reported mood or wellbeing information you enter in the app.
- App settings and preferences, such as theme or feature toggles.
- Device or usage-related data needed for app functionality, if you enable the related feature in Settings.

MoodSense only collects optional feature data when that feature is turned on by you.

## How information is used

MoodSense may use your information to:

- provide core app features;
- personalize your experience;
- generate mood or wellbeing insights;
- maintain account access;
- save your settings and preferences;
- improve app performance;
- respond to support requests;
- help keep the app secure.

## Permissions and optional access

Some features may require Android permissions or system access. If you enable an optional feature, MoodSense may ask for the permission needed for that feature to work.

You can change permissions at any time in Android system settings, though some features may stop working or become less accurate if permission is removed.

## Data sharing

MoodSense does not sell your personal data.

If MoodSense uses third-party services such as Firebase Authentication or hosting, those services may process limited information only as needed to support the app.

## Data storage and security

MoodSense stores information only as long as needed to provide the app, maintain account access, or meet legal or security requirements.

Mood data is stored in Firebase Realtime Database and is associated with your account. Data is retained until you delete it manually or delete your account.

Reasonable security measures are used to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## User choices and controls

You may be able to review, update, or manage parts of your information through the app’s settings or your account profile, depending on the feature.

You can also disable optional data sources from the app settings or from Android system settings.

## Data deletion

You can delete all your mood data from Settings → Delete my data. You can permanently delete your account from Settings → Delete account. Account deletion removes your authentication record. Associated mood data stored in the database is also deleted when you request it.

## Children

MoodSense is not intended for children unless specifically designed and disclosed for that audience.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. When updated, the revised version will be posted with a new date.
